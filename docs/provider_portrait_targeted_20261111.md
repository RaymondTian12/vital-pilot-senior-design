# Individual provider portrait research — November 11, 2026

Added **46 verified portraits**, increasing coverage from **216 to 262/644 (40.7%)**. **382 remain unresolved**. All original 216 entries are unchanged. The original 42-provider demo subset now has **27/42** portraits.

## Scope and evidence

All **428** previously unresolved providers received an individual full-name/city search, ordered Arlington, Dallas, then the remaining saved inventory order. Additional exact-NPI/name searches and links from official pages resolved selected ambiguous cases. **None of the 428 were skipped**, but the searches were not exhaustive across the public web.

Official candidates were identified for **428 providers**. The pass made **211 profile-page checks** (177 loaded), plus **143 published-image URL checks** (117 downloaded and decoded successfully). Downloads include responsive variants and duplicate candidates; they are not distinct accepted portraits. Selected additions were visually inspected in contact sheets or individually.

The [fixed inventory](provider_portrait_inventory.json) retains each query, official sources checked, profile/image outcomes and identity evidence. Original directory searches and earlier research remain intact. Publishers supplied the image URLs; no filename was guessed.

Exact official NPIs were used when available. Otherwise, matching required the full name, specialty and CMS practice address or phone. Published structured physician-photo data was used for HCA profiles. Image-serving query parameters such as `file` and `url` identify different assets and are preserved in duplicate checks; cache and size parameters do not.

Known location changes do not turn a portrait into evidence of current employment or appointment availability. Dennis Battock and Gordon Mitchell have retired/emeritus notices on the inspected official sources; their portrait verifies identity only. Current provider search still determines which records are returned.
